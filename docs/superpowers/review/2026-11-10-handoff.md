# The tiny planet: hand-off

Branch `tiny-planet`. Written 2026-11-12 at the end of the build, for Kylee.
Most important first; the long lists are at the end.

## What was built

The portfolio is now one interactive 3D page: a small planet with five
districts (The Works, Tiny Frenzy, After Hours, The Studio, Home) and nineteen
places, each with its own landmark modelled in code from a shared kit (a
lighthouse for Beacon, a castle for Kingdoms & Crowns, your house with your
five pets for Home, an easel and a drafting table carrying your own art, and
so on). The sky follows the visitor's clock through morning, afternoon,
evening and night; the camera flies to a place when it is clicked or toured,
and the place's panel opens beside it with the project, gallery or contact
form. Every place has its own address (`/places/<id>`), its panel reads and
its links work before the 3D code has loaded or if it fails, and a picture of
the planet stands in where WebGL is missing. The old pages redirect to their
places. The contact form and its backend are unchanged.

## How to look at it

```bash
npm install
npm run dev          # http://localhost:3000
```

For the real thing (what visitors get): `npm run build && npm start`. Try
`/places/home`, the tour (Take the tour), the Index, the Night button, a phone
width, and your system's reduced-motion setting (everything holds still).
The review pictures are in `docs/superpowers/review/landmarks/`.

## Performance, as measured

Measured on 2026-11-12 with all nineteen landmarks, in Chrome on this desktop
(NVIDIA RTX 4060, hardware drawing), against a production build on localhost,
median of three runs:

| Setup | Readable (first paint) | Interactive (first frame + a drag) | Frame rate, orbit / at a place | Headroom (no frame cap), orbit / at a place |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Desktop 1440×900, full quality | 89 ms | 1.0 s | 57 / 57 fps | 516 / 1,041 fps |
| Desktop 1440×900 at 2× pixels | 86 ms | 1.0 s | 57 / 57 fps | 349 / 417 fps |
| Desktop 1440×900, low quality | 91 ms | 0.97 s | 57 / 57 fps | 407 / 867 fps |
| Phone-sized window (412×915), low quality | 86 ms | 0.94 s | 57 / 57 fps | not measured |

The display here runs at about 57 Hz, so 57 fps is "every frame". The
landmarks together draw 332,062 triangles in 475 draw calls (budgets 380,000
and 760).

**Limits:** this is one fast desktop only. **No real phone or weak graphics
card was measured**; the phone row is a phone-sized window on this desktop's
card. The outline shells and the landmark load should be re-checked on a real
phone after launch (Vercel Speed Insights will show real visitors' numbers).

## Known shortfalls

- The landmarks are clean cel-shaded models rather than painterly: the spec's "frame from an anime film" bar is approached, not reached.
- Several landmarks read weakly from straight above (the district view): the flags, the mountain, the civic hall (mostly roof).
- The city's background buildings are all one recipe; the towers near Ruckus read as stacked boxes.
- The label for a place that sits under the open panel loses its tag and pin (for example Three things, seen from Home).
- Shimmer of the brickwork and the rain in motion was checked only in still frames; please look at the Jimothy & Pidge alley live.
- Off the clock now stands on the eastern shore; its stage reaches down to the beach.

## Smaller things deferred along the way

- Content: the `facts` field on projects is unused (no copy yet); the `Modal` component and the projects' `embedNote` field are unused code.
- Labels and HUD: the legend plate is about 716 px wide on a 768 px tablet with its text bunched left; a district tag's first 38 px of leader line is not checked against landmarks; no hysteresis between the zoom fade and the label tier; dismissing the index leaves `#index` in the address; the 404 page has no skip link and its copy is in the component; the "Take the tour" button's accessible name includes its arrow; shown constellation names are read out inside the places nav.
- Camera and tour: pinch-out at a place does not leave it on touch screens; a little leftover orbit damping carries across a flight.
- Landmarks: the House has only about 190 triangles to spare and the Alley 1 draw call; the medium dog's head outline reads slightly as a collar; the pets' markings do not read at the place view; the egg reads as a ghost and the Doll's face is boxy; snow barely separates from rock at night; the d20 has no numbers.
- Engineering: `next-env.d.ts` points at whichever build folder was built last (a typecheck after deleting `.next-e2e` fails until the next build); the poster shows the afternoon or night planet at every hour; about 110 KB of poster is fetched even when the 3D scene replaces it within a second; `lib/world/labels.ts` (about 560 lines) and `LabelProjector.tsx` are large; the buried-part check costs several seconds of the unit suite; a few test helpers rely on react-three-fiber internals.
- Dependencies: `package-lock.json` has some npm-version churn; confirm `npm ci` on CI's Node 20 before the cut-over.
